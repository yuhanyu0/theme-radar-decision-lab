## Frozen before evaluation

Question: under a fixed finite-grammar leverage question, compare first native READY, fixed K=3 paid acquisitions, and B=6 paid acquisitions. 128 synthetic worlds (32 per regime); generator seed 20260925. Truth (net debt, positive EBITDA, leverage ceiling) is sampled BEFORE reports. Correctness is scored against latent world parameters using Fraction arithmetic, not the last report or a gate. Four deliberately different, non-empirical source environments: persistent initial report (initial accuracy .8); improving report accuracy [.55,.65,.75,.85,.9,.95]; deteriorating accuracy [.95,.9,.85,.75,.65,.55]; noisy mixed delivery (accuracy .8). This designed mixture does not estimate a market population.

Every policy shares the same prefix, parser, native WorkOrder, evidence pool, order, linkage, market and source-independence convention. Attempts count as cost even if redundant, irrelevant, future or conflicting. At step 1 a valid report is supplied. For other steps, ordinary regimes sample valid .70 / duplicate .20 / irrelevant .10. Mixed samples valid .45 / duplicate .20 / irrelevant .10 / future .10 / same-revision conflict .15. Valid reports independently sample one of four source families. The first eligible report of a family counts as independent; subsequent versions/copies do not. Those are fixed synthetic annotations, not a real-world independence detector.

All default native WorkOrder requirements and thresholds are retained (all ten industrial dimensions, global sources=2, per-company=1, usable linkage required). A common explicitly synthetic background record supplies the other financial dimensions; it is not marked independent. Net-debt/EBITDA is derived from G1-visible numeric content and placed in typed evidence payloads, then cited by the native company submission. An observed unknown/conflict creates a native UNRESOLVED finding. No truth or expected answer enters a native builder, parser or policy. The common linkage is a declared native-test synthetic fixture, not empirical OHLCV evidence.

All disclosures deemed usable are published before 2026-09-20T19:00:00Z. Acquisition times begin 2026-09-20T20:00:00Z and increase strictly. Future disclosures are unavailable even when acquired. Root D0 contains common background only; every subsequent research batch is explicitly CLOSED for ALL policies, irrespective of accuracy. This fixes execution-closure decisions rather than learning them. All observed archive nodes are retained and only each observed prefix goes to native readiness/admission. No branch is silently discarded.

First READY stops at its first native READY in the supplied prefix; if none by B it defers at B. Fixed and maximum rules stop at their budget and use the SAME native admission requirement, not a shortcut. Primary outcomes: correct, wrong, deferred, plus reason-specific readout UNKNOWN/CONFLICT and native no-admission flags, acquisitions. Shadow factual judgments are retained even if no admission. More reports can improve, have no effect or hurt. No policy success is an integrity gate.

Abstract loss: wrong=1, correct=0, defer=.25, plus .02 per acquisition. Sensitivity grid: defer={.1,.25,.5,1}, acquisition={0,.01,.02,.05,.1}. These are chosen abstract units, NOT dollars or portfolio returns. Report per regime; any pooled summary is the artificial equal mixture only. No p-value, optimality, alpha, agent-generalization or economic-benefit claim. Native action scores are secondary; no Decision hash or tie-driven action reversal is a correctness endpoint.
